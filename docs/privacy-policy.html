Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Thank you for using Ethiopian Calendar ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use our services.

Introduction

Ethiopian Calendar is designed to provide Ethiopian calendar functionality, including:

  • Viewing Ethiopian calendar dates
  • Converting dates between Ethiopian and Gregorian calendars
  • Calculating age based on selected dates
  • Displaying calendar-related information

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

Ethiopian Calendar does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal information.

The App operates entirely on your device and does not require user registration or account creation.

Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Device identifiers
  • Financial information
  • Health information
  • Usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers

How the App Works

All date conversions, calendar calculations, and age calculations are performed locally on your device.

No user data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Internet Access

The App is designed to function without requiring the transmission of personal data over the internet.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data collection services that collect personal information from users.

Data Sharing

Because the App does not collect personal information, no personal information is shared with third parties.

Data Retention

Since no personal information is collected or stored, there is no user data retained by the App.

Children's Privacy

Ethiopian Calendar does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Because the App does not collect any personal information from any users, it is safe for general audiences, including children.

Security

The App performs its functionality locally on the user's device and does not maintain databases of user information.
